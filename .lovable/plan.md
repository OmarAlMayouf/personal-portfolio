

# Omar's Portfolio Website 🇸🇦

A bold, dynamic single-page portfolio with subtle Saudi-inspired design touches (warm sand/desert tones as accents, geometric patterns inspired by Arabic art) on a modern layout.

## Design Direction
- **Bold & Dynamic** style with strong gradients, smooth scroll animations, and eye-catching hero section
- Color palette: Deep navy/dark tones with warm gold/amber accents (subtle nod to Saudi identity)
- Subtle geometric patterns inspired by Arabic/Islamic art used as decorative elements
- Modern typography with clean hierarchy

## Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- Large greeting with your name "Omar" and title "Full Stack Developer"
- Animated subtitle mentioning your coop at Takamol Holding
- CTA buttons: "View My Work" and "Contact Me"
- Subtle background pattern with geometric Arabic-inspired shapes

### 2. About Me
- Brief bio — coop student, based in Saudi Arabia, passionate about full-stack development
- Professional photo placeholder
- Quick stats (6 months experience, technologies used, etc.)

### 3. Experience
- Timeline-style layout showing your coop role at Takamol Holding
- Key responsibilities and achievements (placeholder text you can customize)
- Tech stack badges (React, Spring Boot, etc.)

### 4. Skills
- Visual skills grid with progress indicators
- Categories: Frontend (React, HTML/CSS, TypeScript), Backend (Spring Boot, Java), Tools (Git, etc.)
- Animated skill bars or cards

### 5. Projects
- Card grid with 3 placeholder projects
- Each card has: image placeholder, title, description, tech tags, and links (GitHub/Live)
- Hover effects for interactivity

### 6. Resume Download
- Section with a CTA button to download your CV (PDF placeholder)
- Brief call-to-action text

### 7. Contact / Footer
- Contact form (name, email, message) — frontend only, no backend
- Social links (LinkedIn, GitHub, email)
- Saudi Arabia location mention
- Clean footer with copyright

## Navigation
- Sticky top navbar with smooth scroll to each section
- Mobile-responsive hamburger menu
